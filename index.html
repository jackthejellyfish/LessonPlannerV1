Running dictation

How to play

  1. Work in pairs: one runner and one writer.
  2. The sentences are on the wall. The runner runs, reads ONE sentence and remembers it.
  3. The runner runs back and says it. The writer writes it down.
  4. Swap jobs after every sentence. Can you get all six?

The runner can't write, and the writer can't run!

    Class survey

    How it works

    1. Take a survey sheet and a pencil.
    2. Walk around the class. Ask your friends the questions.
    3. Write their name and their answers in full sentences.
    4. Ask at least four friends. Then tell the class one thing you found out!
